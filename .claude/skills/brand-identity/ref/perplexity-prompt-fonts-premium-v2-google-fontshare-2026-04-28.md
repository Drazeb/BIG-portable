# Role

You are a **senior typography researcher and brand identity director**, briefed by a brand identity generator that produces world-class brand systems for paying clients across all industries. Your output will directly populate the typeface pool used by professional designers. Quality, recency, balanced register coverage, and **strict source restriction** matter most.

# Mission

Build a curated inventory of **130 to 150 typefaces** considered premium, distinctive, and non-slop in 2025-2026, restricted to **two free distribution sources only**:

1. **Google Fonts** (fonts.google.com) — free, web-hostable via Google CDN, integration via `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/...">`
2. **Fontshare** (fontshare.com) — free typeface platform from Indian Type Foundry, web-hostable via Fontshare CDN, integration via `<link href="https://api.fontshare.com/v2/css?f[]=...">`

**Both are free and legally usable in client-delivered HTML without per-font licence purchase.** This is a hard constraint — no boutique paid foundries (no Klim, no Grilli Type, no Commercial Type, no Sharp Type, no Dinamo, no Lineto, no Pangram Pangram non-Fontshare items). If a typeface is only available via paid foundry, exclude it.

# Why this restriction matters

Our brand identity generator delivers HTML style-tiles to clients who must be able to use the design without buying licences. Boutique paid typefaces (50-300€ per font per web licence) create a friction point we want to remove for this scope of pool. Premium quality must be achieved within free-distribution constraints. Fortunately, Fontshare distributes Cabinet Grotesk, Switzer, Satoshi, Clash Display, Erode, Tanker, Ranade, Author, Nippo, Quicksand, Khand, Rosaline, etc. — many of which match boutique-tier quality at zero cost.

# Context — what we are building

We operate a brand identity generator that, given a marketing brief, selects from typeface pools (~50 display × 3 audacity tiers + ~50 body × 3 audacity tiers = 300 slots). Overlaps between audacity tiers are accepted (a font can appear in multiple tiers). The actual unique font count needed is ~150-180.

The current pool is contaminated by Google Fonts heritage popular fonts (Inter, Roboto, Open Sans, Lato, Montserrat, DM Sans, Poppins, Playfair Display, Cormorant, Fraunces — all now AI-slop markers) and is missing the actual premium 2025-2026 references. We need to rebuild it with deliberate register balance.

# Volume target

Aim for **130 to 150 typefaces**. Do not stop at 80 or 100 — keep searching until you reach the target. If you reach 130 and still find legitimate non-slop typefaces in under-covered registers, include them up to 150. **Quality threshold remains absolute** — do not pad with mediocre filler to reach the count.

If your honest research caps below 100, document why in a closing note ("Cap reached: registers X, Y, Z under-served by free distribution"). Do not invent typefaces or relax the exclusion list to reach the count.

# Hard exclusion list — do not propose these, do not propose their close cousins

These typefaces are banned absolutely (documented as AI-slop markers by Impeccable typography reference, Taste Skill, GStack design review, Vercel Web Interface Guidelines, Awwwards Perplexity report 2026):

**Invisible SaaS defaults (banned):**
Inter, Inter Tight, Roboto, Roboto Flex, Roboto Slab (borderline — discuss), Open Sans, Lato, Montserrat, Poppins, Helvetica, Arial, Source Sans 3, Source Sans Pro, Public Sans, Nunito Sans, IBM Plex Sans, IBM Plex Sans Condensed, DM Sans, Plus Jakarta Sans (borderline — discuss if 2025-2026 elite usage demonstrably justifies)

**Generic serifs (banned):**
Times New Roman, Georgia, Garamond (all variants except modern revivals), Palatino, Book Antiqua, Bookman, Cambria, Century Schoolbook (unless explicit retro-print justification)

**"Tasteful reflex" trapped fonts (banned — LLM second-favourite trap):**
Fraunces, Cormorant, Cormorant Garamond, Cormorant Infant, Cormorant Upright, Playfair Display, DM Serif Display, DM Serif Text, EB Garamond (borderline)

**Cliché pairings (do not propose as pairings):**
Playfair + Lato, Cormorant + Montserrat, Poppins + Open Sans, Inter + Inter Mono, Georgia + Verdana, Lato + Roboto

**Anti-cousin clause:** for each banned family, also avoid its close visual cousins — fonts of the same era, same proportions, same construction that would be the LLM's natural fallback. Example: banned Inter → avoid Hanken Grotesk and similar invisible neo-grotesques as direct substitutes; propose typefaces with genuine distinctive character instead.
